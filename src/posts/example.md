Posts are written in Markdown. Drop `.md` files into `src/posts/` and they appear automatically.

## Frontmatter

Each post requires a YAML frontmatter block at the top:

```yaml
---
title: "Your post title"
date: "2026-01-15"
description: "One-sentence summary shown in the post list."
---
```

The filename (without `.md`) becomes the URL slug. For example, `src/posts/my-first-post.md` will be at `/blog/my-first-post`.
